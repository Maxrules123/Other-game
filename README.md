# Credit Card Stealer Simulator / Clubhouse Update

A fictional Roblox arcade simulator with a fully rebuilt city, staged card-snatching challenges, deeper equipment progression, and persistent clubhouse improvements. All cards, banks, and currency are invented.

## Download and play

1. On this repository's **Clubhouse Update branch**, click the green **Code → Download ZIP** button.
2. Extract the downloaded ZIP.
3. Open **CreditCardStealer.rbxl** in Roblox Studio using **File → Open from File**.
4. Press **Play**. The map is generated by the server when testing starts.

Use this branch's build, not an earlier downloaded place. The source project is at the repository root. `START-HERE.md` has Rojo setup steps. The separate `CreditCardStealer.zip` includes the same place, XML place, source folder, and verification manifest.

## What changed

- **Five districts rebuilt around actual places.** Fifteen enterable shops have open entrances, interiors, windows, signs, awnings, roof details, and different furnishings. Crumb Mart has stocked shelves; Bubble Laundry has washers and waiting seats; office/bank lobbies have reception areas; cafes have tables and counters.
- **City spaces with distinct uses.** Starter has Crumb Square, Shopping Plaza has a three-stall food court, Business has a transit concourse, Luxury has a fountain garden, and Neon has a card-sculpture exchange plaza. Crosswalks, delivery vans, drains, bus shelters, planters, alleys, dumpsters, crates, and back-lane housing add street detail.
- **Reasons to explore.** Connected alleys and back lanes lead to extra collector targets and fifteen permanent city souvenirs with individual server-validated rewards and journal clues.
- **45 targets and 15 guards.** Named NPCs have four personalities, different accessories, short walk routes, pauses, limb motion, suspicion colors, and target previews. There are back-lane patrols as well as street patrols.
- **Better stealing.** Scout first, choose Careful or Bold, then clear one to three timing stages. Stages include forward, reverse, and bouncing cursors with later-stage drifting zones. Every phase has a fresh server-owned token; the server awards one card only after the complete trick.
- **Risk and mastery.** Bold trades smaller/faster windows and extra Heat for card value and rarity luck. Perfect runs improve rarity; consecutive completed tricks add capped card-quality bonuses. Misses, live cancellations, capture, and death break the streak. No client-provided scores or reward amounts are accepted.
- **Nine equipment tracks.** Composure, Gadgets, and Appraisal join the original six. The shop shows tier ranks, current/next effects, progress bars, Cash costs, and Reputation requirements. Appraisal's weighted luck still matters in endgame districts without Common drops.
- **Confetti decoy.** Buy Gadgets, restock at a clubhouse supply bench, then use G / R1 / the touch button outside. Charges, cooldown, Heat relief, and per-player pursuit suppression are validated by the server. Workshop improvements increase charge capacity.
- **Crumb Club.** Each district has an enterable safe room with banking, gear, supplies, a three-stage risk-free practice arcade, lounge, gallery, missions, and route board. Personal client-side decorations reflect your discoveries, identity, and room upgrades without changing another player's room progress.
- **Permanent home progression.** Lounge, Workshop, Collector Gallery, and Courier Network each have three levels. They improve recovery, supplies, banking, and unlocked-district travel. Home upgrades, souvenir discoveries, and career stats persist across New Identity.
- **Old progress retained.** Schema 2 adds missing fields to schema-1 profiles in the same store. It keeps existing Cash, equipment, unlocks, collection, and mission progress. Session leases, retries, autosaves, load failure safeguards, and separate Studio test profiles remain in place.

The existing collection, daily/starter missions, Vault Run, prestige, capture rules, and menus remain connected to the expanded loop. See `UPGRADE-GUIDE.md` for effects and formulas, and `TESTING.md` for verification and pending Roblox checks.

## First things to try

1. Walk out of Crumb Club and scout a Daydreamer. Choose Careful and finish the starter timing challenge.
2. Bank at the terminal at the back of the club and buy Sleight. Continue the guided objective banner.
3. Visit the stocked mart and laundry, follow an alley behind the shops, and discover a gold souvenir.
4. Find a Collector or Fancy VIP. Try the reverse/bouncing stages; switch to Bold once comfortable.
5. Use the gold practice arcade for a three-stage run without Heat or rewards.
6. Buy Gadgets, refill at the mint supply bench, then use a decoy while chased.
7. Improve the clubhouse. View personal trophies and the city journal in **Hideout**; unlock Courier Network and use the route board with empty pockets and low Heat.

## Settings, assets, and limitations

- Default Studio sessions use fresh, nonpersistent profiles. Published servers use production saving. Read START-HERE before changing API settings.
- `Config.StoreName` intentionally stays `CCSS_Production_v1` while the profile schema becomes 2, so existing players can migrate without losing progress. An older schema-1 server rejects newer profiles instead of overwriting them.
- Carried cards, live streak, and decoy charges are session state. Bank Cash before leaving; profile autosaves are every 60 seconds, with best-effort shutdown saves. A crash can lose progress since the latest successful save.
- Configure permitted audio in Config.Audio. All slots remain empty, so this delivery has no music or sound effects yet. No external models, images, or asset IDs are needed by the city.
- The city uses Roblox primitives and programmatic actor motion. Satellite clubhouses share a structural layout; personal decoration upgrades are displayed in the Starter clubhouse. There is no player-built housing editor or trading system.
- Timing uses server receipt time minus Roblox-reported ping capped at 180 ms. High-latency feel needs device tests. These reward checks are not comprehensive protection against client movement exploits or automated input.
- Set published maximum players to 12. World/NPC/security logic runs at bounded intervals; pathfinding is throttled to two concurrent requests. Profile performance in Studio with the expanded 45 targets and 15 guards before release.
